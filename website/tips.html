Emergency Safety Tips

  • Always have a list of emergency contacts saved on your phone.
  • On most smartphones, pressing the power button five times quickly will start a series of emergency actions like calling for help.
  • Know how to quickly dial emergency services (e.g., 111 in NZ, 911 in the US, 112 in the EU).
  • In addition to Check-in Chicken, you can use live location sharing with trusted contacts when traveling alone in unfamiliar places through google maps.
  • Stay aware of your surroundings when walking alone, especially at night.
  • Plan your route in advance, choosing well-lit, busy streets over isolated areas.
  • Wait for public transport in well-lit, populated areas, if possible.
  • Be cautious about sharing your location or travel plans on social media such as instagram or facebook.
